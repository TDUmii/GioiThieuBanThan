# Dấu mốc tạo nên tôi

The portfolio keeps the agreed warm memory-album visual world: cream paper, dark navy ink, muted brick red, sage, pale sky and honey-yellow tape. Personal photos remain the central artifacts. No synthetic personal photos or invented dates, names, awards or travel locations.

The hero displays the camera portrait in a correctly sized, gently rotated photograph with a small floral memory behind it. The title uses local Quicksand and a restrained Patrick Hand handwritten phrase, with Vietnamese glyph coverage verified against the page content. The introduction uses open rows instead of a cluster of equal cards. Four chronological chapters alternate copy and full-image frames along a quiet timeline. The travel gallery respects landscape and portrait images. The animal section uses a full-width navy surface with a warm framed photo. The ending returns to paper.

## Type and layout

Self-hosted Quicksand 500/700 and Patrick Hand from Google Fonts, with OFL licenses in static/fonts. Display sizes stay at or below 6rem and tracking at or above -0.035em. Main layout is 1160px maximum, with phone layouts at 580px and a tablet navigation breakpoint at 820px. Image heights are explicitly responsive, and buttons never inherit the source image's full pixel height.

## Behavior

Photos open in a native dialog with previous/next, keyboard arrows and focus restoration. Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript modes keep all content readable. Mobile navigation is hidden from focus when closed. Section links preserve normal URL anchors. UI assets and fonts are served locally.

## Content boundary

Source copy lives in profile_data.py. School, STEM, university, defense, travel and animal image mappings remain unchanged. No contact channels are invented. Repository publication stays private and contains only this project.
